Direct response, in Taglish

now/ (direct_response, quality_gate)

Today I am the Direct Response Copywriter and Quality Lead at Amare Group Inc., the e-commerce company behind Zenith and Luxi. I write the ads: hooks, video scripts, landing pages, emails, influencer briefs, mostly in Taglish, sometimes in Bisaya. And I run the quality gate. Every script, page and brief on the team passes my desk before it ships, and when I change something I write down why, tied to the data. Each week I read hook rate, hold rate, CTR and CPA, and the next round of copy changes because of what they say.

  • Write: ad scripts and hook stacks for UGC, influencer, customer interview and street interview formats, plus the social copy that runs with them
  • Convert: landing pages for cold traffic, email flows and campaigns in Klaviyo, offer and page tests
  • Gate: review all team copy before launch, weekly creative reviews, the team's quality standards doc
  • Read: hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA and contribution margin, turned into the next test
